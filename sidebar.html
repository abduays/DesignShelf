About DesignShelf
DesignShelf stores captured snippets in local extension storage. It does not upload captures to a server. Captures can include visible page text, URLs and design assets, so review exports before sharing them.
Entered form values are excluded from captures. Only capture designs you have permission to inspect and reuse. Respect site licences, copyright and privacy obligations.
